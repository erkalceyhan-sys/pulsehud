Experience your device's raw power with PulseHUD — the ultimate live wallpaper and real-time system monitor.

PulseHUD visualizes your phone's heartbeat with ultra-smooth 60 FPS GPU-accelerated HUD themes.

• **Real-Time CPU Pulse:** Live processor frequency and dynamic load waveforms.
• **RAM & Storage Telemetry:** Monitor active memory allocation and free disk space.
• **Network Throughput:** Instant download/upload speeds and ping latency.
• **Battery Thermal Sensor:** Keep an eye on battery percentage and operating temperature.
• **Stunning Futuristic Themes:**
  - *Cyberpunk Neon:* High-tech electric glow and rotating radar arcs.
  - *Obsidian Minimalist:* Pure OLED black with clean Apple-style activity rings.
  - *Matrix Terminal:* Classic green digital rain and Linux telemetry.
  - *Arc Reactor:* Sci-Fi rotating reactor responding dynamically to phone workload.
• **Battery Friendly:** Built on high-performance native engines with zero idle background battery drain.
