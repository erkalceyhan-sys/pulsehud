# Privacy Policy for PulseHUD

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction
PulseHUD ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles device data and telemetry.

## 2. On-Device Processing
All hardware metrics (including CPU usage, RAM consumption, network throughput, battery temperature, and storage indicators) are read and rendered **entirely locally on your device**.
- No hardware data or personal information is ever transmitted to external servers.
- We do not track, collect, or store your personal identity, browsing history, or private files.

## 3. Advertising & Analytics
We may use standard advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. These providers may collect anonymous advertising identifiers subject to their respective privacy policies.
